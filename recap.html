I was born in Calgary, Alberta in 1992. As I grew up I became one of the artsy kids on the fringes of the punk scene and decided I'd become a photographer.

In 2011 I moved to Vancouver, British Columbia to attend Douglas College after being rejected from the Parsons photo program for my abysmal high school grades.

After a year and a half at Douglas, a school I still remember fondly, I managed to transfer to Eugene Lang College at the New School. It was the fall of 2013 and I was finally in New York City.

Not long after, I became roommates with a bike messenger and mechanic. Through a strange series of events we opened a bike shop called Haven Cycles in 2016. I was 23.

On the job at Haven I got a crash course in working on bikes and running a busy business in Brooklyn. It was one of the most challenging, confusing, and rewarding times of my life.

In 2019, I left Haven. In 2020, when COVID hit, I tried to return. Bikes were the most essential thing I could think of and the only way I could help. But it was time for me to leave Haven permanently.

By the end of 2020 I was out and looking for the perfect location for a new bike shop. Bedford-Stuyvesant was sorely lacking. On April 1, 2021, Bike Plant officially opened.
