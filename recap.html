I was born in Calgary, Alberta in 1992. Growing up, I was extremely shy. I became one of the artsy kids on the fringes of the punk scene and decided I'd become a photographer.

In 2011 I moved to Vancouver, British Columbia to attend Douglas College after being rejected from the Parsons photo program for my abysmal high school grades.

After a year and a half at Douglas, a school I still remember fondly, I managed to transfer to Eugene Lang College at the New School. It was the fall of 2013 and I was finally in New York City.
